# Content Rating Guidance

This guide helps prepare the Google Play content rating questionnaire.

## Suggested category answers

- Violence: No
- Sexual content: No
- Profanity: No
- Controlled substances: No
- Gambling: No
- **Contains ads: YES** — AdMob banner, interstitial, and app-open ads are
  shipped in the current version. The Play Console "Ads" declaration must
  be set to **Yes** (mismatch here is a direct rejection reason).
- User-generated content: Yes, only private notes created by the user
- Online interaction: No public social feed in current version
- Location sharing: No, unless geofenced reminders are enabled later
- Digital purchases: No, unless subscriptions/in-app purchases are added later

## Target audience

Recommended: 13+ or general productivity audience.

The app is not designed for children under 13.

## Important

Ads are already shipped (declared above). If future versions add public
sharing, collaboration, AI-generated content publishing, purchases, or
location features, update the questionnaire accordingly.
