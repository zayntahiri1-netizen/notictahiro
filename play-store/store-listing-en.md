# Notic Tahiro - Google Play Store Listing English Draft

## App name

Notic Tahiro

## Short description

AI productivity notebook for notes, ideas, tasks, finance, and debt tracking.

## Full description

Notic Tahiro is a smart productivity notebook designed to turn daily chaos into a clear, organized system.

Capture notes, save ideas, organize projects, track debts and credits, and record finance entries in a simple workflow. The app includes AI-inspired tools for note summaries, task extraction, smart tagging, daily planning, financial reminders, and productivity insights.

## Key features

- Create, edit, and manage notes and ideas.
- Organize content with projects and tags.
- Switch note layout between square and rectangle views.
- Tahiro AI center for productivity insights and daily plans.
- Debt and credit tracker with proactive reminders.
- Smart finance tracker for expenses and investments.
- Knowledge graph to visualize connected ideas.
- Fast search and semantic chat with notes.
- Supports Arabic, English, Spanish, and Chinese.
- Clear privacy and data deletion pages.

## Disclaimer

Finance and debt features are for personal organization only. The app does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
