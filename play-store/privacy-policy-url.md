# Required Public URLs

For Google Play review, in-app pages are useful, but the Privacy Policy must be available as a public HTTPS URL.

After deploying to Firebase Hosting, use your real domain.

## Recommended URLs

If using Firebase default hosting:

- Privacy Policy: `https://notictahiro.web.app/privacy`
- Delete Data: `https://notictahiro.web.app/delete-data`
- About: `https://notictahiro.web.app/about`
- Contact: `https://notictahiro.web.app/contact`

If using Firebase alternate domain:

- Privacy Policy: `https://notictahiro.firebaseapp.com/privacy`
- Delete Data: `https://notictahiro.firebaseapp.com/delete-data`

## Play Console fields

Use the public privacy URL in:

- App content > Privacy policy
- Data Safety > Data deletion URL if requested
- Store listing contact email: `zayntahiri1@gmail.com`

## Requirements

- The URL must be public.
- The URL must not require login.
- The page must mention app name.
- The page must mention contact email.
- The page must match the Data Safety form.
